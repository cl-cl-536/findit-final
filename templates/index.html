How It Works

Three straightforward steps designed to safely reunite campus members with their personal belongings.

1

1. Report an Item

Submit a quick 2-minute report for something you misplaced or discovered. Upload a photograph, campus location, and description.

2

2. Browse Lost and Found Items

Filter by category, search by building or department, and review photo listings to identify matches instantly.

3

3. Reunite with Belongings

Connect directly with the finder or campus security desk, verify identity, and mark the report resolved.
